Apps Marketplace · Concept Review
Product design exploration

Three directions for the Apps marketplace.

These concepts explore how enabled apps, self-serve tools, premium demos, future waitlists, and product suggestions could work together in the Connect the Doc experience.

These are early sketches created for internal discussion and alignment. Content, visuals, interactions, and product behavior are not final.
Concept 01

Compact Marketplace

A familiar app-store structure that keeps installed and self-serve apps compact while giving premium products more visual space.

  • Compact app grid
  • Full-width premium billboards
  • Simple state legend

Concept 02

Editorial Marketplace

A single-page experience where every app has room to explain its value through focused copy, benefits, and product imagery.

  • Individual app sections
  • Persistent sidebar navigation
  • Strong product storytelling
View concept→
Concept 03

Featured Marketplace

A discovery-first layout that prioritizes premium apps while keeping installed tools available through a focused My Apps area.

  • Premium apps shown first
  • Collapsible installed apps
  • Marketplace status navigation
